# Moving

[中文](move.md) · [日本語](move.ja.md) · [한국어](move.ko.md)

A seated lord, general, or advisor orders it for themselves. They walk one city along a road linked to where they are. It does not spend the 3 domestic points, and it adds none. It excludes march, duel, and hold. One war order remains on the slip.

When the order is given, both the city they leave and the city they enter must be held by their own faction. Resolution checks again. If the city has changed hands, the road is not adjacent, the person has died or been captured, or they are no longer in the city where they gave the order, the step is void. They stay where resolution finds them. They do not cross an enemy city, and they do not take another step from a city they were scattered to. The troops stay behind.

Within one turn the order is: domestic work (still on the city where the order was given) → diplomacy → disaster and refugees → march and the scatter after a city falls → the move. Inside one faction, the lord does not approve each step. An empty seat, or another living player, cannot be moved by someone else. Changing faction still needs the seated lord who receives them.

Locking the slip only seals it. The harvest, the result of an assault, and a city's color play after the chronicle has written them. A refresh does not play a turn that was already seen. Motion can be on, less, or off. The game is playable with no video. The grain-check concept clip is not a production spectacle.
